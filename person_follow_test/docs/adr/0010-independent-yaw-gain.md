# ADR 0010: Independent yaw gain
User confirms good pitch and requests slightly less yaw lag. Sampled video logs control48Hz, vision16-18Hz; no evidence of yaw-specific bus stall. Add yaw_gain_multiplier default1, validated0.5..2, deployed1.3. Multiply yaw visual-error gain only, leaving pitch arithmetic identical and all trajectory derivative/rate bounds unchanged. This increases unsaturated yaw response, not camera FPS or guaranteed latency; fast direction reversal may overshoot. Regression checks pitch outputs remain identical and yaw response improves at moderate image error. Board validation remains required.
